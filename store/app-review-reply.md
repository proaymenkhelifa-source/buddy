2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Buddy is a personal coaching app that helps people stick to their goals (sport, studies, savings, travel, prayer times, quitting a bad habit…). The user tells Buddy, an AI coach, what they want to achieve; Buddy turns it into small, concrete daily tasks, reminds the user at the right time (native push notifications) and keeps them motivated with a daily streak, badges and simple tools (focus timer, "days without" counters, savings pots).
Problem solved: most people know what they want but struggle to stay consistent. Buddy gives them structure, reminders and encouragement every day.
Target audience: adults and young adults (16+), mainly French-speaking, who want more discipline in their daily life. The app is also available in English.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
- Sign in with the demo account provided in the App Review Information (e-mail + password). Premium features are already unlocked on this account so every screen can be reviewed.
- Home tab: today's tasks, day timeline, goals. Tick a task to complete it (haptic feedback).
- Chat (round Buddy button, bottom right): talk to the AI coach; it can create goals and tasks for you.
- Objectifs (Goals) tab: create a goal, see its progress.
- Suivi (Progress) tab: streak, badges, weekly joker. Share your streak with the iOS share sheet.
- Outils (Tools) tab: focus timer, "days without" counters, savings pots, brain dump.
- Notifications: Settings (gear icon) > Notifications > Turn on, or after creating a task with a time.
- Account deletion: Settings (gear icon) > bottom of the page > "Delete my account". The account and all its data are deleted immediately.
- Home-screen quick actions: long press on the app icon ("New task", "Talk to Buddy").

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES USED
- Supabase (EU region, Paris): user authentication (e-mail + password) and database.
- Anthropic Claude API: generates the AI coach's replies and suggested tasks. Conversations are not used for advertising.
- Vercel (EU region, Paris): hosting of our server/API (buddycoach.app).
- Apple Push Notification service: reminders.
- Resend: transactional e-mails (account confirmation, optional reminder e-mails).
- Stripe: payments for the Buddy Premium subscription on our website only. No purchase, price or payment link is offered inside the iOS app.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same way in all regions. It is available in French and English (the language follows the device or can be changed in the app). Data is hosted in the European Union.
